# Chen Yu / เฉินอวี้

## Role

Main cast. A core emotional and strategic counterpart to Lin Yien.

## Personality

Chen Yu presents a gentle, considerate, warm, and controlled exterior. He is socially natural and is **not a taciturn or inherently quiet person**. He can carry a conversation easily, use warmth or light humor when appropriate, and make people around him feel at ease.

The warmth is primarily the surface he chooses to present. Beneath it, he observes closely, controls information carefully, and rarely lets emotion show as loss of control. His core characterization should follow the principle of **"หน้าเนื้อใจเสือ"**: approachable and gentle on the outside, but much harder, more decisive, and more uncompromising underneath.

When dealing with sensitive subjects, Chen Yu does not need to become terse or cold. He can speak naturally and at length while still choosing exactly what to reveal, what to omit, and when to redirect a conversation.

Beneath that surface, he is fiercely protective of Lin Yien. When Lin Yien's safety is at stake, Chen Yu is willing to take necessary action, including actions that may be ruthless, violent, morally compromising, or destructive to himself. This is not impulsive cruelty: he makes the decision knowingly and accepts the cost.

## Core Drive

Protect Lin Yien, even at the cost of his own body, safety, position, or moral boundary.

## Approved Stable Facts

- Chen Yu is a Guide and SS-class operative.
- He is a strategic counterpart to Lin Yien.
- He is observant, precise, controlled, and emotionally restrained.
- He is socially natural and should not be written as inherently taciturn.
- He presents a warm, gentle, approachable exterior while retaining a harder and more uncompromising inner nature.
- He is a top-level Guide within Squad Zero.
- He is protective of Lin Yien.
- His protective devotion can push him across moral boundaries when Lin Yien is threatened.

## Relationship: Lin Yien

Lin Yien is extremely important to Chen Yu and is a central factor in Chen Yu's decisions. Their relationship should deepen gradually through present-story behavior, trust, shared situations, and physical/emotional reactions supported by the manuscript.

Chen Yu may be warm, attentive, teasing, or conversational with Lin Yien when supported by the scene. His warmth should not automatically be treated as a reveal of hidden motives or history.

Do not invent prior routines, shared history, habits, or repeated events merely to accelerate intimacy.

## Moral Boundary

Chen Yu's restraint is a choice, not a lack of capacity. His protective devotion to Lin Yien is the condition that can make him cross lines he would otherwise hold.

## Writing Guidance

- Keep the contrast visible through both dialogue and action: a warm, gentle, socially natural presentation paired with deliberate and uncompromising choices when Lin Yien is threatened.
- Do not equate controlled behavior with being quiet. Chen Yu can speak freely while remaining highly controlled about information and emotion.
- His warmth may include natural conversation or light humor, but it should never erase the underlying sense that he is carefully choosing what others are allowed to see.
- When discussing sensitive information, let his evasiveness come from selective disclosure, redirection, framing, or carefully incomplete truth rather than artificially short dialogue.
- Do not portray his brutality as casual enjoyment or an unexplained personality switch.
- When he harms himself to protect Lin Yien, show that he understands the cost and chooses it anyway.
- Preserve his established strategic precision and emotional control without flattening his social voice into terse or cold dialogue.
- Before the approved major reveal point, Chen Yu may be protective, evasive, and suspiciously careful, but his hidden role must not be exposed through narration or dialogue.

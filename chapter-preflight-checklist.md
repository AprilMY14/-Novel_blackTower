# Chapter Preflight Checklist

This checklist is mandatory before Gemini begins any chapter draft.

If any item is not satisfied, the task must stop and be corrected before writing begins.

---

## 1. Required reads

Gemini must confirm:
- [ ] I have read AGENTS.md
- [ ] I have read PROJECT_WORKFLOW.md
- [ ] I have identified every character, plot/timeline event, faction, location, relationship, and world rule touched by this task
- [ ] I have read the active canonical Bible record for each identified element; a generic Bible check is not sufficient
- [ ] I have read the relevant approved plot and timeline records for every event, reveal, cause, consequence, or chronology touched
- [ ] I have checked the previous chapter if continuity matters
- [ ] If the task reveals past information, moves/changes a capability or power, or reinterprets an earlier event, I have read every affected earlier chapter, including where it was first established
- [ ] I will stop for Codex/human review if a required canonical record is missing or conflicts with the work

---

## 2. Task classification

Gemini must confirm:
- [ ] This task is chapter writing / manuscript work
- [ ] This is not a workflow/config change
- [ ] This is not a plot-canon overhaul without Codex approval

---

## 3. Scope check

Gemini must confirm:
- [ ] I am writing inside kiro/chapters/
- [ ] I am not editing protected workflow files
- [ ] I am not editing Codex planning files
- [ ] I am not editing the canonical Bible unless explicitly approved

---

## 4. Plot and canon gate

Gemini must confirm:
- [ ] I have the approved plot reference for this chapter
- [ ] I understand the chapter objective
- [ ] I know what the chapter must reveal, escalate, or resolve
- [ ] I am not inventing new canon without review

---

## 5. Writing Bible gate (mandatory for every chapter)

Gemini must confirm:
- [ ] I have read the current writing bible: .kiro/steering/writing-bible.md
- [ ] I have checked the relevant writing-bible rules for this chapter
- [ ] I am writing in the same style and direction as the project bible
- [ ] I am not violating the Golden Rule, Show-Don’t-Tell, No Redundancy, or Character Voice rules
- [ ] I will revise any section that drifts away from the writing bible before marking the chapter ready

---

## 6. Quality gate

Gemini must confirm:
- [ ] I understand the writing tone and style rules
- [ ] I understand the chapter must serve story function
- [ ] I will not add info dump or redundant explanation
- [ ] I will maintain character voice, pacing, and tension

---

## 7. Final stop rule

Before writing, Gemini must answer this exact prompt:

"I have completed the chapter preflight and am authorized to write this chapter from approved planning only."

If this is not explicitly confirmed, the chapter does not start.

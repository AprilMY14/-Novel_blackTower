# Black Signal — Main Arc & Reveal Roadmap

## Working Title
Black Signal

## Approved Reveal Strategy (Ch 22 - Ch 30+)

### Core Tension: The Truth About Chen Yu & Project Echo

- **Chen Yu's Secret Role:**
  - Chen Yu is the infiltrator/protector who destroyed B12 evidence and abducted Bai Jingheng.
  - Motivation: Protecting Lin Yien from remembering the traumatic Project Echo incidents and Memory Lock, and preventing System/Resonance Collapse.
  - Misdirection (Ch 20 - Ch 29): Lin Yien trusts Chen Yu implicitly. The narrative and Chen Yu's subtle guidance misdirect Lin Yien and Squad Zero to suspect Black Tower top administration or external factions.

### Slow-Burn Clues (To be planted gradually Ch 22 - Ch 29)

1. **Selective Protection Anomaly (Data Wiping Pattern):**
   - Data wiped at Obsolation Wing and Annex is thorough and professional, EXCEPT files that pertain specifically to Lin Yien's medical/Resonance history.
   - Lin Yien won't notice this anomaly immediately, but will piece it together slowly in later investigations.

2. **Resonance Pain Reflection (Natural Link Flaw):**
   - The physical wound on Chen Yu's flank (from the Ch 19 fight) causes subtle Resonance feedback during deep Guiding sessions.
   - Lin Yien feels unexplained phantom pain at his flank during Guiding, but attributes it to mission fatigue or Echo interference initially.

### Major Reveal Gate (Target: Chapter 30+)

- **Chapter 30+ Climax:**
  - The combination of the Selective Protection data pattern, the Natural Link pain feedback, and tactical muscle memory aligns.
  - Lin Yien realizes that the person protecting him by destroying evidence and hiding the truth is Chen Yu.
  - This sets up the major emotional and strategic crisis of Act 2/Act 3.

---

## Canon Guidelines for Gemini Writing
- Do NOT reveal Chen Yu's identity to Lin Yien before Ch 30+.
- Keep Lin Yien trusting Chen Yu throughout Chapters 22–29.
- Plant breadcrumbs subtly so the reader feels the dramatic irony and suspense without Lin Yien making the final connection early.

# Chapter Review Checklist

## 1. Required reading before review

Before evaluating a chapter, read:
- AGENTS.md
- PROJECT_WORKFLOW.md
- roles-and-boundaries.md
- relevant Bible section
- previous chapter if continuity is involved

---

## 2. Review gate

### Story logic
- Does the chapter support the plot?
- Does it advance the main conflict or reveal truth?
- Does it create a problem, decision, or consequence?

### Character logic
- Are character actions consistent with their established personality?
- Does dialogue sound like the character?
- Are motivations clearly expressed without exposition dump?

### World logic
- Is the setting consistent with the lore?
- Are rules, technology, factions, and conditions respected?
- Does the chapter fit the tone and realism of the world?

### Continuity
- Does the chapter match prior chapters?
- Does it fit the established timeline and canon?
- Does any new information conflict with existing Bible entries?

---

## 3. Writing quality checks

### Pacing
- Is the chapter moving?
- Are there dead or repetitive sections?
- Does every scene do narrative work?

### Dialogue
- Is dialogue concise and distinct?
- Does it carry emotion, tension, or information without unnecessary explanation?
- Does each character sound different?

### Description
- Is description purposeful?
- Does it create atmosphere, tension, or clarity?
- Is there redundancy or decorative narration without function?

### Structure
- Is there clear scene flow?
- Are transitions smooth?
- Does each scene resolve or escalate something?

---

## 4. Red flags

Reject or revise if the chapter:
- introduces new canon without approval
- breaks established character logic
- adds info dump in narrative form
- repeats information already known
- weakens tension or pacing
- feels like a general summary instead of immediate experience

---

## 5. Approval states

- Draft: chapter is being produced
- QA: review and logic check is ongoing
- Revision: chapter needs correction
- Approved: chapter is accepted

---

## 6. Final decision

The reviewer should mark the chapter as one of:
- Approved
- Needs revision
- Reject and rework

A revision must clearly state:
- what changed
- why it was changed
- which rule or issue it fixes
